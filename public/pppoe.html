Set up your router

  1. Connect the MikroTik port 5 cable to the Tenda router’s WAN/Internet port.
  2. Open the Tenda internet/WAN settings and choose PPPoE (not Dynamic IP).
  3. Enter the username and password shown after payment, then save and connect.

The PPPoE connection receives a dynamic IP from CAPTYN. Devices connected to the Tenda share this one paid router subscription.
